# 01_refund_full_en: Online refund, all facts given (English)

**User:**

I paid 4500 for a mixer on Flipkart from XYZ Store on 10 September. It was defective, I returned it on 20 September, but no refund yet. It was for home use.

**Assistant:**

**Where you stand**
You bought a mixer for personal use, which makes you a consumer (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). Because you returned a defective product and have not received your refund, you have a valid claim against the seller [DEFECT, ECOM_SELLER_REFUND]. You have about 22 months left to file a complaint (CP Act 2019, s.69).

**What you can do**
1. Send the seller a written notice asking for your refund with a deadline, though the Act does not legally require this.
2. Register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline by calling 1915 or using consumerhelpline.gov.in.
3. File a complaint online at e-jagriti.gov.in with your bill and order details [STEP_COMPLAINT, E_FILING]. There is no filing fee, and it goes to the District Commission where the value paid is up to Rs 5 lakh [DISTRICT_LIMIT, FEE_NIL].

**Keep ready**
* Purchase bill and order details
* Messages or proof of return with the seller
