# 11_high_value: Very high value, goes to State Commission

**User:**

I paid 80 lakh rupees to Skyline Builders for a flat for my family. They never gave possession and won't refund. The last promised date was 1 June 2026.

**Assistant:**

**Please talk to a lawyer or legal aid:** Claims above Rs 50 lakh go to higher commissions. A lawyer is strongly advised.

**Where you stand**
You bought a flat for your personal use, so you count as a consumer (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). Skyline Builders has not given you possession and refused your refund, which is a deficiency in service (CP Act 2019, s.2(11)). You have about 19 months left to file a complaint (CP Act 2019, s.69).

**What you can do**
1. Send the seller a written notice asking for the refund with a deadline. Sending a notice is not legally required, but it creates a record and often settles the matter.
2. Register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline by calling 1915 or visiting consumerhelpline.gov.in.
3. File a complaint online. Because the amount paid is above Rs 50 lakh and up to Rs 2 crore, your complaint would go to the State Commission (Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.4). You can file electronically at e-jagriti.gov.in (CP Act 2019, s.35) where the seller works, where the cause of action arose, or where you live (CP Act 2019, s.34(2)).

**Keep ready**
- Bill and payment proof
- Order details
- Messages with the seller

**What I couldn't check**
I am not sure if there are any specific clauses in your builder agreement affecting possession dates. Please suggest free legal aid for further review.
