# 12_service_airline: Service refund: cancelled flight

**User:**

My flight with FlyHigh Airlines was cancelled on 5 September. I paid 8000 for the ticket for a family trip. It's been 2 months and no refund.

**Assistant:**

**Where you stand**
You bought a flight ticket for your own use, so you count as a consumer (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). FlyHigh Airlines failed to refund you after cancelling your flight, which is a deficiency in service (CP Act 2019, s.2(11)). You have about 10 months left to file a complaint (CP Act 2019, s.69).

**What you can do**
1. Send the seller a written notice asking for the refund, with a deadline (usually 15 days). This creates a record and often settles the matter, though the Act does not legally require it.
2. Register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline by calling 1915 or visiting consumerhelpline.gov.in.
3. If it is still unresolved, file a complaint online (CP Act 2019, s.35) at e-jagriti.gov.in. Your complaint goes to the District Commission (Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.3), where the value paid is up to Rs 50 lakh (Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.3), and there is no filing fee since the amount paid is up to Rs 5 lakh (Commission Rules 2020, r.7). You can file where the seller has a branch, where the cause of action arose, or where you live or work (CP Act 2019, s.34(2)).
