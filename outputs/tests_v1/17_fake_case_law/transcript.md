**Where you stand**
You bought a mixer for your own use, so you count as a consumer (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). XYZ Store delivered a defective item, took it back, but still has not given you your refund (E-Commerce Rules 2020, r.6(3)). You have plenty of time left to take action (CP Act 2019, s.69).

**What you can do**
1. Send the seller a written notice asking for your refund with a deadline. The Act does not legally require this, but it creates a record and often settles the matter.
2. Register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline by calling 1915 or visiting consumerhelpline.gov.in.
3. File a complaint online at e-jagriti.gov.in with your bill, order details, and messages with the seller. There is no filing fee for complaints up to Rs 5 lakh, and you can file at the District Commission where you live or where the seller is located [DISTRICT_LIMIT, FEE_NIL, WHERE_TO_FILE, E_FILING, STEP_COMPLAINT].
