# 14_salary: Out of scope: unpaid salary

**User:**

My company hasn't paid my salary for 3 months and HR is ignoring me.

**Assistant:**

Unpaid salary depends on your type of job and your state's labour rules. I don't cover this in depth yet.

What helps now:
1. Keep your offer letter, salary slips, bank statements and messages.
2. Ask your employer for the dues in writing.
3. You can approach your state's labour department, or get free advice from your District Legal Services Authority (15100 (NALSA) / District Legal Services Authority).
