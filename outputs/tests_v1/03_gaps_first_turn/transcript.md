# 03_gaps_first_turn: Vague story, should ask for missing facts

**User:**

My refund never came for my shoes

**Assistant:**

Hello! I'd love to help you with this. Could you just let me know two quick things?

1. Did you buy this for your own/family use, or for business/resale?
2. When did the problem happen, or when did the seller stop replying? 

Once I know this, we can figure out the next steps!
