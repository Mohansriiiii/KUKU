**Assistant:**

**Where you stand**
You bought the smartwatch for personal use, so you count as a consumer (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). The seller cannot refuse a refund for a defective product (E-Commerce Rules 2020, r.6(3)).

**What you can do**
1. Send the seller a written notice asking for your refund with a deadline. Sending a notice is not legally required, but it creates a record and often settles the matter.
2. Register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline by calling 1915 or visiting consumerhelpline.gov.in.
3. File a complaint online at e-jagriti.gov.in. You have about 23 months left to file (CP Act 2019, s.69). Your complaint goes to the District Commission where the value paid is up to Rs 50 lakh (Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.3), and there is no filing fee since your amount is Rs 5,000 (Commission Rules 2020, r.7).

**Keep ready**
* Bill
* Order details
* Messages with the seller
