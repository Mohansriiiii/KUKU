# 07_refund_hinglish: Refund in Hinglish

**Assistant:**

**Where you stand**
You bought a kurta for personal use, so you count as a consumer (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). Trendz gave you the wrong size and kept your money, which breaks the rules for online sellers (E-Commerce Rules 2020, r.6(3)). You have about 23 months left to file a complaint (CP Act 2019, s.69).

**What you can do**
1. Send Trendz a written notice asking for your refund with a deadline. The Act does not require this, but it creates a record and often settles the matter.
2. Register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline by calling 1915 or visiting consumerhelpline.gov.in.
3. If it is still unresolved, file a complaint at the District Commission (Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.3). You can file online at e-jagriti.gov.in (CP Act 2019, s.35), and there is no filing fee for complaints up to Rs 5 lakh (Commission Rules 2020, r.7).

**Keep ready**
* Bill
* Order details
* Messages with the seller

**What I couldn't check**
I am not sure if Trendz has responded to your return through any official channel other than not refunding you. If you need more help, you can consult free legal aid.
