# 09_late_claim: Offline purchase, 2-year limit has passed

**User:**

I bought a TV from Sharma Electronics, a local shop, for 30000 in March 2024. It stopped working in April 2024 and the shop refused to repair it. It was for my home.

**Assistant:**

**Please talk to a lawyer or legal aid:** The 2-year limit passed 221 days ago. A late complaint needs a good reason for the delay. Talk to a lawyer or legal aid.

**Where you stand**
You bought a TV for personal use, which makes you a consumer (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). Because the TV stopped working and the shop refused to repair it, you face a product defect (CP Act 2019, s.2(10)). You are well within the two-year time limit to file a complaint (CP Act 2019, s.69).

**What you can do**
1. Send the seller a written notice asking for the repair or refund with a deadline. Sending a notice is not legally required, but it creates a record and often settles the matter.
2. Register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline by calling 1915 or visiting consumerhelpline.gov.in.
3. If it remains unresolved, file a complaint at the District Commission because your paid amount is up to Rs 50 lakh (Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.3). You can file it online at e-jagriti.gov.in (CP Act 2019, s.35), and there is no filing fee since your paid amount is up to Rs 5 lakh (Commission Rules 2020, r.7). You can file where the seller has a branch or where you live (CP Act 2019, s.34(2)).

**Keep ready**
* Purchase bill
* Records of your communication with the shop

**What I couldn't check**
I am not sure if any written warranty terms affect your repair rights. Please consider free legal aid for further guidance.
