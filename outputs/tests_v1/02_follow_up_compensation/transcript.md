# 02_follow_up_compensation: Follow-up question after the explanation

**User:**

I paid 4500 for a mixer on Flipkart from XYZ Store on 10 September. It was defective, I returned it on 20 September, but no refund yet. It was for home use.

**Assistant:**

**Where you stand**
You bought a mixer for personal use, which makes you a consumer (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). XYZ Store cannot refuse your refund because you returned a defective product [DEFECT, ECOM_SELLER_REFUND].

**What you can do**
1. Send the seller a written notice asking for the refund with a deadline (usually 15 days). Sending a notice is not legally required, but it creates a record and often settles the matter.
2. Register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline by calling 1915 or using consumerhelpline.gov.in.
3. File a complaint online at e-jagriti.gov.in. Because the amount paid is Rs 4,500, there is no filing fee (Commission Rules 2020, r.7). You can file your complaint at the District Commission where the seller works, where the issue happened, or where you live [WHERE_TO_FILE, E_FILING].

**Keep ready**
* Bill and order details
* Messages or proof of return with XYZ Store

**What I couldn't check**
I am not sure if there are any specific policy terms or delay reasons not mentioned here; if you face issues, please suggest free legal aid.

**Sources:** CP Act 2019, s.2(7); Commission Rules 2020, r.7

**User:**

Can I get compensation also, not just the refund?

**Assistant:**

Yes, you can get compensation.

You count as a consumer because you bought the mixer for your own use (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). A defective mixer is considered a defect (CP Act 2019, s.2(10)).

If your complaint succeeds, the commission can order the seller to return the price and pay compensation (CP Act 2019, s.39) [CPA_S39_p1].

**Sources:** CP Act 2019, s.2(7); CP Act 2019, s.2(10); CP Act 2019, s.39
