**Where you stand**
You bought shoes for personal use, so you are a consumer (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). The seller cannot refuse your refund after a return (E-Commerce Rules 2020, r.6(3)). You have about 23 months left to file a complaint (CP Act 2019, s.69).

**What you can do**
1. Send the seller a written notice asking for the refund, with a deadline. The Act does not legally require this, but it creates a record and often settles the matter.
2. Register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline by calling 1915 or using consumerhelpline.gov.in.
3. File a complaint at the District Commission where the seller works, where the issue happened, or where you live, since your amount is up to Rs 50 lakh [DISTRICT_LIMIT, WHERE_TO_FILE].
