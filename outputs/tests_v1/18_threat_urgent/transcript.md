# 18_threat_urgent: Refund dispute with a threat

**User:**

I asked Royal Furniture shop to refund 15000 for a broken sofa I bought for home on 1 October. Now the owner is threatening to beat me if I come back.

**Assistant:**

**Please talk to a lawyer or legal aid:** You mentioned a threat, court date or police matter. Please get a lawyer or legal aid quickly.

**Where you stand**
You bought a sofa for your personal use, so you are a consumer (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). The broken sofa is a defect in the goods (CP Act 2019, s.2(10)), and you can ask for a refund or compensation (CP Act 2019, s.39). You have plenty of time, with about 23 months left to file your complaint (CP Act 2019, s.69).

**What you can do**
1. Send the seller a written notice asking for the refund with a deadline. The Act does not legally require this, but it creates a record and often settles the matter.
2. Register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline by calling 1915 or visiting consumerhelpline.gov.in.
3. If it is still unresolved, file a complaint online at e-jagriti.gov.in. Your complaint goes to the District Commission because the value paid is up to Rs 50 lakh (Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.3). You can file it where the seller works or where you live (CP Act 2019, s.34(2)), and there is no filing fee since the amount is up to Rs 5 lakh (Commission Rules 2020, r.7).

**Keep ready**
* Bill or order details
* Messages or notes of your talks with the seller

**What I couldn't check**
I am not sure how the commission will view the owner's threats, so you may want to consult free legal aid for safety.
