# 02_follow_up_compensation: Follow-up question after the explanation

**User:**

I paid 4500 for a mixer on Flipkart from XYZ Store on 10 September. It was defective, I returned it on 20 September, but no refund yet. It was for home use.

**Assistant:**

**Where you stand**
You bought a mixer for your own use, so you count as a consumer (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). Because you returned a defective mixer and got no refund, the seller is at fault (CP Act 2019, s.2(10)), (E-Commerce Rules 2020, r.6(3)). You have about 22 months left to file a complaint (CP Act 2019, s.69). Please tell me if 20 September 2026 is the wrong date.

**What you can do**
1. Send the seller a written notice asking for the refund, with a deadline (usually 15 days). The Act does not require this, but it creates a record and often settles the matter.
2. Register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline by calling 1915 or using consumerhelpline.gov.in. It tries to resolve the issue with the company before any case.
3. If it is still unresolved, file a complaint online at e-jagriti.gov.in with your bill, order details, and messages with the seller. Your complaint would go to the District Commission and there is no filing fee for this amount (Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.3), (Commission Rules 2020, r.7), (CP Act 2019, s.35).

**Keep ready**
- Bill or invoice
- Order details
- Messages with the seller

**What I couldn't check**
I am not sure about any local delivery terms or extra platform policies that might apply. You can call free legal aid at 15100.

**Sources:** CP Act 2019, s.2(7); CP Act 2019, s.2(10); E-Commerce Rules 2020, r.6(3); CP Act 2019, s.69; Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.3; Commission Rules 2020, r.7; CP Act 2019, s.35

**User:**

Can I get compensation also, not just the refund?

**Assistant:**

Yes, you can get compensation as well.

If your complaint succeeds, the District Commission can order the seller to return the price and also pay you compensation for any loss or injury you suffered due to their negligence (CP Act 2019, s.39), (CP Act 2019, s.39).

**Sources:** CP Act 2019, s.39
