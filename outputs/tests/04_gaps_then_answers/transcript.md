I'm sorry to hear that your refund for the shoes never came! 

To understand your situation better, could you please tell me:
1. Did you buy these shoes for your own/family use, or for a business/resale?
2. When did you buy them, or when did the seller refuse/stop replying to you?

**Assistant:**

Thanks for sharing that—buying shoes for ₹2,200 on Myntra from the seller Footwear Hub helps a lot. 

Could you please let me know when you returned the shoes (or when the refund was supposed to come), and when the seller stopped replying or refused your refund?

**User:**

I returned them on 1 October. They were for me.

**Assistant:**

**Where you stand**
You bought shoes for personal use, so you count as a consumer (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). Footwear Hub cannot refuse to refund your money for returned goods (E-Commerce Rules 2020, r.6(3)). You have about 23 months left to file a complaint, counted from 01 October 2026. Please tell me if that date is wrong (CP Act 2019, s.69).

**What you can do**
1. Send the seller a written notice asking for the refund, with a deadline. The Act does not require this, but it creates a record and often settles the matter.
2. Register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline by calling 1915 or using consumerhelpline.gov.in.
3. If it is still unresolved, file a complaint online at e-jagriti.gov.in. Your complaint goes to the District Commission (Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.3), where there is no filing fee for this amount (Commission Rules 2020, r.7). You can file it electronically (CP Act 2019, s.35).

**Keep ready**
- Your bill or order details
- Messages with the seller

**What I couldn't check**
I am not sure about delivery delays caused by sudden unavoidable events. You can call free legal aid at 15100.

**Sources:** CP Act 2019, s.2(7); E-Commerce Rules 2020, r.6(3); CP Act 2019, s.69; Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.3; Commission Rules 2020, r.7; CP Act 2019, s.35
