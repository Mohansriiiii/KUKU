**Please talk to a lawyer or legal aid:** Claims above Rs 50 lakh go to higher commissions. A lawyer is strongly advised.

**Where you stand**
You bought a flat for personal use, so you count as a consumer (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). Skyline Builders kept your money and did not give you possession or a refund. You have about 19 months left to file a complaint, counted from 01 June 2026. Tell me if that date is wrong (CP Act 2019, s.69).

**What you can do**
1. Send the seller a written notice asking for the refund, with a deadline. The Act does not require this, but it creates a record and often settles the matter.
2. Register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline by calling 1915 or using consumerhelpline.gov.in.
3. File a complaint online at e-jagriti.gov.in. Your complaint goes to the State Commission (Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.4). You can file it electronically (CP Act 2019, s.35).
