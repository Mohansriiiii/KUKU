**Where you stand**
You bought a mixer for personal use and returned it because it was defective. XYZ Store has not given you your refund yet (CP Act 2019, s.2(7); CP Act 2019, s.2(10); E-Commerce Rules 2020, r.6(3)).

**What you can do**
1. Send the seller a written notice asking for the refund, with a deadline. The Act does not legally require this, but it creates a record and often settles the matter.
2. Register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline by calling 1915 or visiting consumerhelpline.gov.in.
3. File your complaint online at e-jagriti.gov.in. Time limit counted from 20 September 2026. Tell me if that date is wrong. About 22 months left to file a complaint (CP Act 2019, s.69; CP Act 2019, s.35). Your complaint goes to the District Commission. There is no filing fee for this amount (Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.3; Commission Rules 2020, r.7).
