**Where you stand**
You bought the smartwatch for personal use, so you count as a consumer (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). The seller cannot refuse a refund when the product is defective (E-Commerce Rules 2020, r.6(3)). **What you can do**
1. Send the seller a written notice asking for the refund, with a deadline (usually 15 days). The Act does not require this, but it creates a record and often settles the matter.
2. Register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline: call 1915 or use consumerhelpline.gov.in. It tries to resolve the issue with the company before any case.
3. If the complaint succeeds, the commission can order the seller to remove the defect, replace the goods, return the price, pay compensation, and in some cases pay punitive damages (CP Act 2019, s.39). File your complaint at the District Commission online at e-jagriti.gov.in (CP Act 2019, s.34(2); CP Act 2019, s.35). There is no filing fee for this amount (Commission Rules 2020, r.7).
