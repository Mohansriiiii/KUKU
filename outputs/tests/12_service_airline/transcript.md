# 12_service_airline: Service refund: cancelled flight

**User:**

My flight with FlyHigh Airlines was cancelled on 5 September. I paid 8000 for the ticket for a family trip. It's been 2 months and no refund.

**Assistant:**

**Where you stand**
You bought a flight ticket for personal use, so you count as a consumer (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). FlyHigh Airlines failed to provide a refund after cancelling your flight, which is a shortcoming in service (CP Act 2019, s.2(11)). You have about 22 months left to file a complaint, counted from 05 September 2026. Please tell me if that date is wrong (CP Act 2019, s.69).

**What you can do**
1. Send the seller a written notice asking for the refund, with a deadline (usually 15 days). The Act does not require this, but it creates a record and often settles the matter.
2. Register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline: call 1915 or use consumerhelpline.gov.in. It tries to resolve the issue with the company before any case.
3. If it is still unresolved, file a complaint online at e-jagriti.gov.in with your bill, order details, and messages with the seller. Your complaint would go to the District Commission, and there is no filing fee for this amount (Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.3; Commission Rules 2020, r.7).

**Keep ready**
- Flight ticket bill
- Order details
- Messages with the seller

**What I couldn't check**
I am not sure if there are any specific airline terms or conditions that apply to your cancellation. You can call free legal aid at 15100.
