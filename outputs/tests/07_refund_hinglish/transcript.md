# 07_refund_hinglish: Refund in Hinglish

**User:**

Maine Meesho se 1200 ka kurta liya tha Trendz seller se, size galat aaya, 25 October ko return kiya par refund nahi aaya. Apne liye liya tha.

**Assistant:**

**Where you stand**
Aapne personal use ke liye kurta kharida aur galat size return karne ke baad bhi refund nahi mila, toh aap ek consumer hain (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). Trendz ke liye yeh refund dena zaroori tha (E-Commerce Rules 2020, r.6(3)). Aapke paas complaint karne ke liye 23 months baaki hain (CP Act 2019, s.69).

**What you can do**
1. Trendz ko ek written notice bhej kar refund maango aur ek deadline do (jaise 15 din). Act ke mutabiq yeh notice dena zaroori nahi hai, par isse ek record ban jata hai.
2. National Consumer Helpline par complaint register karo: 1915 par call karo ya consumerhelpline.gov.in use karo.
3. Agar fir bhi baat na bane, toh e-jagriti.gov.in par online complaint file karo. Yeh complaint District Commission mein jayegi (Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.3), jahan iska koi filing fee nahi hai (Commission Rules 2020, r.7), aur aap ise online bhi file kar sakte hain (CP Act 2019, s.35).

**Keep ready**
- Bill aur order details
- Seller ke sath ki gayi chats ya messages

**What I couldn't check**
Mujhe yeh pata nahi hai ki 25 October 2026 ki date sahi hai ya nahi. Agar aapko koi aur madad chahiye ya kuch samajh na aaye, toh aap free legal aid ke liye 15100 par call kar sakte hain.

**Sources:** CP Act 2019, s.2(7); E-Commerce Rules 2020, r.6(3); CP Act 2019, s.69; Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.3; Commission Rules 2020, r.7; CP Act 2019, s.35
