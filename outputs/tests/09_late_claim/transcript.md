**User:**

I bought a TV from Sharma Electronics, a local shop, for 30000 in March 2024. It stopped working in April 2024 and the shop refused to repair it. It was for my home.

**Assistant:**

**Please talk to a lawyer or legal aid:** The 2-year limit passed 221 days ago. A late complaint needs a good reason for the delay. Talk to a lawyer or legal aid.

**Where you stand**
You are a consumer since you bought the TV for your personal use (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). The TV stopping to work is a defect in goods (CP Act 2019, s.2(10)), but the 2-year limit to file passed 221 days ago (CP Act 2019, s.69).

**What you can do**
1. Send the seller a written notice asking for the repair or refund, with a deadline. The Act does not require this, but it creates a record and often settles the matter.
2. Register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline by calling 1915 or using consumerhelpline.gov.in.
3. If still unresolved, file a complaint at the District Commission (Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.3), where the value paid is up to Rs 50 lakh (Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.3). There is no filing fee (Commission Rules 2020, r.7). You can file online at e-jagriti.gov.in (CP Act 2019, s.35).
