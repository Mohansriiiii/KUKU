**Where you stand**
You bought a sofa for your own use, so you count as a consumer (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). A broken sofa counts as a defect (CP Act 2019, s.2(10)). You must file your complaint within 2 years from when the issue started on 01 October 2026 (CP Act 2019, s.69). Please tell me if that date is wrong.

**What you can do**
1. Send the seller a written notice asking for the refund, with a deadline. The Act does not require this, but it creates a record.
2. Register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline by calling 1915.
3. File a complaint online. There is no filing fee for this amount (Commission Rules 2020, r.7). Your complaint would go to the District Commission (Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.3). You can file it online at e-jagriti.gov.in (CP Act 2019, s.35).
