# 09_late_claim: Offline purchase, 2-year limit has passed

**User:**

I bought a TV from Sharma Electronics, a local shop, for 30000 in March 2024. It stopped working in April 2024 and the shop refused to repair it. It was for my home.

**Assistant:**

**Please talk to a lawyer or legal aid:** The 2-year limit passed 221 days ago. A late complaint needs a good reason for the delay. Talk to a lawyer or legal aid.

**Where you stand**
You bought the TV for your personal use, so you count as a consumer (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). The TV stopping to work is a defect, and the shop refusing to repair it is unfair (CP Act 2019, s.2(10)).

**What you can do**
1. Send the seller a written notice asking for the refund, with a deadline. The Act does not legally require this, but it creates a record and often settles the matter.
2. Register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline by calling 1915 or using consumerhelpline.gov.in.
3. If it is still unresolved, file a complaint online (CP Act 2019, s.35) at e-jagriti.gov.in. The 2-year limit to file passed 221 days ago, so a late complaint is accepted only if the commission accepts a good reason for the delay (CP Act 2019, s.69). Your complaint goes to the District Commission (Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.3). There is no filing fee for this amount (Commission Rules 2020, r.7).
