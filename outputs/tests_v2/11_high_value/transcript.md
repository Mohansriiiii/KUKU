**Assistant:**

**Please talk to a lawyer or legal aid:** Claims above Rs 50 lakh go to higher commissions. A lawyer is strongly advised.

**Where you stand**
You bought a flat for your own personal use, so you count as a consumer (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). Skyline Builders did not give you possession and refused to refund your money, which is a service deficiency (CP Act 2019, s.2(11)). You must file your complaint within 2 years from 01 June 2026 (CP Act 2019, s.69). Tell me if that date is wrong.

**What you can do**
1. Send the seller a written notice asking for the refund with a deadline. The Act does not legally require this, but it creates a record and often settles the matter.
2. Register a grievance by calling 1915 or using consumerhelpline.gov.in.
3. If unresolved, file your complaint online at e-jagriti.gov.in (CP Act 2019, s.35). Your complaint goes to the State Commission because your amount is above Rs 50 lakh and up to Rs 2 crore (Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.4).
