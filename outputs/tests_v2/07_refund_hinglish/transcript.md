# 07_refund_hinglish: Refund in Hinglish

**User:**

Maine Meesho se 1200 ka kurta liya tha Trendz seller se, size galat aaya, 25 October ko return kiya par refund nahi aaya. Apne liye liya tha.

**Assistant:**

**Where you stand**
Aap consumer ho kyunki aapne kurta personal use ke liye kharida tha (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). Trendz ne refund nahi diya, jo ki galat hai (E-Commerce Rules 2020, r.6(3)). Aapke paas complaint karne ke liye abhi lagbhag 23 mahine baaki hain (CP Act 2019, s.69).

**What you can do**
1. Aap seller ko ek written notice bhej sakte hain refund maangne ke liye. Yeh Act ke mutabiq zaroori nahi hai, par isse ek record ban jata hai.
2. Aap National Consumer Helpline par 1915 par call karke ya consumerhelpline.gov.in par shikayat darj kar sakte hain.
3. Agar baat na bane, toh aap District Commission mein complaint file kar sakte hain (Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.3). Iska online e-filing e-jagriti.gov.in par kiya ja sakta hai (CP Act 2019, s.35), aur iske liye koi fee nahi lagti hai (Commission Rules 2020, r.7).

**Keep ready**
- Bill aur order details
- Seller ke saath ki gayi chat ya messages
- Wrong size aur return ki details

**What I couldn't check**
Mujhe yeh confirm nahi hai ki 25 October 2026 ki date bilkul sahi hai ya nahi. Agar aapko koi aur madad chahiye, toh aap free legal aid ke liye 15100 par call kar sakte hain.
