**Where you stand**
You bought a mixer for personal use, which makes you a consumer (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). A seller on an online marketplace cannot refuse a refund if the goods are defective (E-Commerce Rules 2020, r.6(3)). You have about 22 months left to file a complaint, counted from 20 September 2026 (tell me if that date is wrong) (CP Act 2019, s.69).

**What you can do**
1. Send the seller a written notice asking for the refund, with a deadline (usually 15 days). Sending a notice is not legally required, but it creates a record and often settles the matter.
2. Register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline: call 1915 or use consumerhelpline.gov.in to try and resolve the issue.
3. If it remains unresolved, file a complaint online at e-jagriti.gov.in with your bill, order details, and messages. Your complaint goes to the District Commission (Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.3), there is no filing fee for this amount (Commission Rules 2020, r.7), and it can be filed electronically (CP Act 2019, s.35).

**Keep ready**
- Bill and order details
- Messages with the seller
