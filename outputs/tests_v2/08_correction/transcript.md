**Where you stand**
You are a consumer since you bought the smartwatch for personal use (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). GadgetPro refusing your refund after a return is against the rules (E-Commerce Rules 2020, r.6(3)).

**What you can do**
1. Send the seller a written notice asking for the refund, with a deadline (usually 15 days). Sending a notice is not legally required, but it creates a record and often settles the matter.
2. Register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline by calling 1915 or using consumerhelpline.gov.in.
3. File a complaint online at e-jagriti.gov.in with your bill and order details (CP Act 2019, s.35). There is no filing fee for this amount (Commission Rules 2020, r.7). Your complaint goes to the District Commission (Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.3).
