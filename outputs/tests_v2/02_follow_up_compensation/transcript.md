# 02_follow_up_compensation: Follow-up question after the explanation

**User:**

I paid 4500 for a mixer on Flipkart from XYZ Store on 10 September. It was defective, I returned it on 20 September, but no refund yet. It was for home use.

**Assistant:**

**Where you stand**
You are a consumer because you bought the mixer for your own use (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). XYZ Store must not refuse a refund since the returned mixer was defective (E-Commerce Rules 2020, r.6(3)).

**What you can do**
1. Send the seller a written notice asking for the refund, with a deadline (usually 15 days). Sending a notice is not legally required, but it creates a record and often settles the matter.
2. Register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline: call 1915 or use consumerhelpline.gov.in.
3. File a complaint online at e-jagriti.gov.in with your bill, order details, and messages. You have about 22 months left to file, counted from 20 September 2026 (CP Act 2019, s.69). Your complaint goes to the District Commission (Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.3), and there is no filing fee for this amount (Commission Rules 2020, r.7).

**Keep ready**
- Purchase bill and order details
- Return confirmation
- Messages with the seller

**What I couldn't check**
I am not sure if your issue date of 20 September 2026 is correct. If you need more help, you can call free legal aid at 15100.

**Sources:** CP Act 2019, s.2(7); E-Commerce Rules 2020, r.6(3); CP Act 2019, s.69; Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.3; Commission Rules 2020, r.7

**User:**

Can I get compensation also, not just the refund?

**Assistant:**

You bought the mixer for your personal use, so you count as a consumer (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)).
The seller cannot refuse to refund you for a defective product (E-Commerce Rules 2020, r.6(3)).
If your complaint succeeds, the District Commission can order the seller to return the price paid and also pay compensation (CP Act 2019, s.39).

**Sources:** CP Act 2019, s.2(7); E-Commerce Rules 2020, r.6(3); CP Act 2019, s.39
