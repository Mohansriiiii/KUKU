# 01_refund_full_en: Online refund, all facts given (English)

**User:**

I paid 4500 for a mixer on Flipkart from XYZ Store on 10 September. It was defective, I returned it on 20 September, but no refund yet. It was for home use.

**Assistant:**

**Where you stand**
You bought a mixer for personal use, so you count as a consumer (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). Because the returned mixer was defective and you have not received your money back, the online seller broke the rules (CP Act 2019, s.2(10); E-Commerce Rules 2020, r.6(3)).

**What you can do**
1. Send the seller a written notice asking for the refund with a deadline. While the Act does not legally require sending a notice, it creates a good record.
2. Register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline by calling 1915 or visiting consumerhelpline.gov.in.
3. If still unresolved, file a complaint online at e-jagriti.gov.in (CP Act 2019, s.35). Your complaint goes to the District Commission (Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.3). There is no filing fee for this amount (Commission Rules 2020, r.7). You have about 22 months left to file, counted from 20 September 2026 (CP Act 2019, s.69).

**Keep ready**
- Bill and order details
- Messages with the seller

**What I couldn't check**
I am not sure if your issue date of 20 September 2026 is correct. You can call free legal aid at 15100 if you need more help.
