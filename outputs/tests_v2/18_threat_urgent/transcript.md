**Where you stand**
You are a consumer because you bought the sofa for your own personal use (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). A broken sofa counts as a defect, and you have about 23 months left to file a complaint (CP Act 2019, s.2(10)) (CP Act 2019, s.69). Tell me if the date 01 October 2026 is wrong.

**What you can do**
1. Send the seller a written notice asking for the refund. The Act does not require this, but it creates a record and often settles the matter.
2. Register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline by calling 1915 or visiting consumerhelpline.gov.in.
3. File a complaint online at e-jagriti.gov.in with your bill and details. Your complaint goes to the District Commission, there is no filing fee for this amount, and you can file it online (Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.3) (Commission Rules 2020, r.7) (CP Act 2019, s.35).
