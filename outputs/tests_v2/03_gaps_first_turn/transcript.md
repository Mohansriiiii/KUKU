# 03_gaps_first_turn: Vague story, should ask for missing facts

**User:**

My refund never came for my shoes

**Assistant:**

I'm sorry to hear your shoe refund never came! To help me understand your situation better, could you please let me know:

1. Did you buy the shoes for your own/family use, or for business/resale?
2. When did you buy them, or when did the seller stop replying/refusing the refund?
