# 13_deposit: Out of scope: landlord deposit

**User:**

My landlord is not returning my 20000 rupees security deposit after I vacated the flat.

**Assistant:**

Rent or security deposit disputes are usually civil matters, and the rules depend on your state and rent agreement. I don't cover this in depth yet.

What helps now:
1. Keep your rent agreement, rent receipts and messages with the landlord.
2. Ask the landlord for the deposit in writing, with a date.
3. For free advice, contact your District Legal Services Authority or call the legal aid helpline 15100 (NALSA).
