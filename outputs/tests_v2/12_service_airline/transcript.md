# 12_service_airline: Service refund: cancelled flight

**User:**

My flight with FlyHigh Airlines was cancelled on 5 September. I paid 8000 for the ticket for a family trip. It's been 2 months and no refund.

**Assistant:**

**Where you stand**
You bought a flight ticket for personal use, so you count as a consumer (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). FlyHigh Airlines failed to provide a refund after cancelling your flight, which is a deficiency in service (CP Act 2019, s.2(11)).

**What you can do**
1. Send the seller a written notice asking for the refund with a deadline (usually 15 days). The Act does not legally require this, but it creates a record and often settles the matter.
2. Register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline by calling 1915 or using consumerhelpline.gov.in.
3. If it remains unresolved, file a complaint online at e-jagriti.gov.in with your bill and messages. Your complaint goes to the District Commission (Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.3), where there is no filing fee for this amount (Commission Rules 2020, r.7). You have about 22 months left to file, counted from 05 September 2026 (let me know if that date is wrong) (CP Act 2019, s.69).

**Keep ready**
- Flight ticket or bill
- Messages or records with FlyHigh Airlines

**What I couldn't check**
I am not sure if there are extra terms in your ticket that apply to cancellations. You can call free legal aid at 15100.
