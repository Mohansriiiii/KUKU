**Where you stand**
You bought shoes for personal use, so you count as a consumer (CP Act 2019, s.2(7)). Footwear Hub cannot refuse your refund after you returned the product (E-Commerce Rules 2020, r.6(3)). You have about 23 months left to file a complaint, counted from 01 October 2026 (CP Act 2019, s.69).

**What you can do**
1. Send the seller a written notice asking for the refund. The Act does not require this, but it creates a record and often settles the matter.
2. Register a grievance with the National Consumer Helpline at consumerhelpline.gov.in or by calling 1915.
3. File a complaint online at e-jagriti.gov.in. It goes to the District Commission, has no filing fee, and can be done electronically (Jurisdiction Rules 2021, r.3; Commission Rules 2020, r.7; CP Act 2019, s.35).

**Keep ready**
- Bill and order details
- Messages with the seller

**What I couldn't check**
I am not sure if your issue date of 01 October 2026 is correct. If you need more help, you can call free legal aid at 15100.
